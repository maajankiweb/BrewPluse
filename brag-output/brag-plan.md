# BrewPluse Launch Video — Brag Plan

## 1. Project Inspection Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   BrewPluse is a modern, high-speed cafe & restaurant operating system with POS billing, live kitchen display (KDS), waiter mobile ordering, and table QR ordering with ₹0/month software fees.

2. **What is the most impressive claim?**
   "Run your cafe. Not your software." — ₹0 monthly retainers, ₹1/order usage meter, and 0% MDR on direct UPI.

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   The dark emerald & teakwood aesthetic (`#0b2b1a`, `#141210`, `#f59e0b`), the physical-feel POS register cards, and the real-time OLED chalkboard TV menu.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   - The interactive POS Register punching table orders with one tap
   - The instant KOT firing to the Kitchen Display System (KDS)
   - Contactless dynamic UPI billing with thermal slip printing

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   20 seconds (5 distinct scenes, punchy rhythm).

6. **What tone fits best?**
   - Preset: `polished` with `app-store` energy.
   - Direction: Sleek, high-energy modern hospitality tech launch.

---

## 2. Storyboard (Total Duration: 20.0s)

### Scene 1: The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s)
- **Visual**: Dramatic dark emerald background with glowing coffee icon. Bold typography reveals: "STILL PAYING ₹30,000/YR FOR CLUNKY POS SOFTWARE?"
- **Motion**: Kinetic punch-in, gentle floating particles.
- **SFX**: Deep riser + bass drop.

### Scene 2: The Reveal (3.5s – 7.5s)
- **Visual**: Introducing BrewPluse. Headline: "MEET BREWPLUSE. ZERO SUBSCRIPTION."
- **Mockup**: Sleek POS iPad screen slides in showcasing the interactive register, category filter tabs, and live cart.
- **SFX**: Smooth whoosh + camera shutter click.

### Scene 3: Highlight 1 — Speed POS & Instant KOT (7.5s – 11.5s)
- **Visual**: One-tap order punching. Order `#BP-1042` fires across the room.
- **Split Screen**: Left shows POS bill; Right shows live Kitchen Display System (KDS) ticket turning from yellow to green with prep timer.
- **Callout**: "Instant Kitchen Sync &bull; Web Bluetooth Thermal Print".
- **SFX**: Double-beep scanner + chime.

### Scene 4: Highlight 2 — Contactless Table QR & UPI (11.5s – 15.5s)
- **Visual**: Teakwood table QR stand mockup. Smartphone scanning dynamic UPI QR code.
- **Callout**: "Guests Scan. Order. Pay via UPI. 0% Commission."
- **SFX**: Cash register chime + digital ding.

### Scene 5: Outro & Punchline (15.5s – 20.0s)
- **Visual**: Full BrewPluse suite lockup (POS + KDS + QR + Inventory).
- **CTA**: "Run Your Cafe. Not Your Software."
- **Badge**: "Free to start &bull; Setup in 5 minutes &bull; brewpluse.com"
- **SFX**: Warm acoustic chord fade-out.
