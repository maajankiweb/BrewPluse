### 1.8 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Deck)
1. **Q: Is BrewPluse really ₹0/month forever? What is the catch?**  
   *A:* There is no catch. We do not charge monthly or annual software subscriptions. All core modules—POS billing, QR table ordering, kitchen KDS, inventory tracking, and staff accounts—are 100% unlocked. We monetize through a nominal ₹1 micro-fee on completed orders and optional hardware sales.
2. **Q: Do I need to buy expensive proprietary hardware to use BrewPluse?**  
   *A:* Absolutely not. BrewPluse is 100% browser-native and runs on any existing iPad, Android tablet, Windows PC, Mac, or smartphone. For physical receipt printing, BrewPluse connects wirelessly to standard 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers via Web Bluetooth or USB.
3. **Q: How does GPS Geofencing protect my cafe from fake QR orders?**  
   *A:* BrewPluse verifies the customer's physical proximity using device geolocation. If someone scans a photo of your QR code from miles away, the order is blocked, ensuring only seated guests can dispatch tickets to your kitchen.
4. **Q: What happens if my cafe's internet drops during rush hour?**  
   *A:* BrewPluse includes offline caching resilience. Your cashier can continue taking manual orders and printing receipts locally; all transactional data automatically synchronizes with the cloud once connectivity is restored.
5. **Q: How do UPI and digital payments get settled into my bank account?**  
   *A:* Payments made via table QR codes flow directly into your registered merchant UPI or bank account through integrated payment gateway partners (Razorpay / Cashfree) with standard T+1 settlements.
