help Frequently Asked Questions
How does live kitchen order tracking work? expand_more
Once your order is confirmed, it is dispatched to the kitchen display screen. You can track real-time preparation timers, plating status, and get audio alerts when your meal is served.
What payment options are accepted? expand_more
We accept Instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit cards via Razorpay, and direct Cash payment at the counter.
Can I customize milk and sweetness? expand_more
Yes! Tap any coffee or tea in the Menu tab to choose Oat milk, Almond milk, sugar level, and add custom instructions for the barista.
