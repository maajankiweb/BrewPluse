# BrewPluse Brand Guidelines & Identity Spec
*Founder Brand Architecture*

## 1. Brand Essence & Mission
- **Brand Name**: **BrewPluse**
- **One-Line Promise**: *"Brew Excellence. Automate Everything."*
- **Primary Tagline**: *"The Free Modern Operating System for Coffee Shops & Cafes."*
- **Secondary Consumer Tagline**: *"Handcrafted Coffee & Artisan Dining."*

---

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Warm & Artisan**: Speaks like a seasoned head roaster who cares deeply about bean origins, milk texturing, and hospitality craft.
- **Direct & Transparent**: Zero corporate jargon or hidden terms. Clear about pricing (₹0/mo software, ₹1 convenience fee).
- **Modern & Reassuring**: Never sterile or bureaucratic; invites cafe owners into a supportive partnership.
- **Word Rules**:
  - Say: *"Table Ordering"*, *"Barista Queue"*, *"Handcrafted"*, *"Zero Lock-in"*.
  - Never Say: *"Omnichannel SaaS Paradigm"*, *"Synergistic POS"*, *"Enterprise Solution"*.

---

---

## 4. Typography & Iconography
- **Primary Display & Body Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Geometric, clean, modern, warm).
- **Monetary & Order Font**: `JetBrains Mono` (Precise, legible for order numbers, thermal receipts, and price figures).
- **System Icons**: Google Material Symbols Outlined (Clean 20px glyphs with gold/green accent fills).
- **Brand Icon**: Official BrewPluse Icon (`/logos/brewpluse-icon.svg`) featuring a gold coffee cup, telemetry bar charts, and an emerald coffee leaf sprout.
