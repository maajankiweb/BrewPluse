# BrewPluse Strategic Pricing Architecture

## 1. The Core Pricing Strategy
BrewPluse employs a **Freemium Platform Disruption Model**:
- **Cafe Owner SaaS**: **₹0 / Month Forever** (Zero monthly retainers, zero annual upfront lock-in).
- **Transaction Monetization**: **₹1 Flat Platform Fee** charged to the patron per QR table order checkout.
- **Hardware & Physical Products**: High-margin branded acrylic QR stands and thermal receipt printers sold to cafes.

## 3. The ₹1 Platform Convenience Fee Rationale
- For a typical order of ₹350–₹700 at a coffee shop, ₹1 represents less than **0.25%** of the bill — virtually unnoticed by diners while replacing 2%–3% credit card/aggregator fees.
- For a cafe processing 120 orders a day, this generates ₹3,600/month in platform micro-revenue directly to BrewPluse with zero collection friction.
