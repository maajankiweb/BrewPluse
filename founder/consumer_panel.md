# BrewPluse 100-Buyer Consumer Panel Simulation

## Executive Summary
A panel of **100 verified cafe founders, general managers, and beverage directors** was simulated across four core segments:
1. **Specialty Coffee Roasteries & Third-Wave Cafes** (25 buyers)
2. **Student & College Hangout Cafes** (25 buyers)
3. **Neighborhood Bakeries & Tea Rooms** (25 buyers)
4. **Fast-Casual QSR & High-Turnover Cafes** (25 buyers)

## 2. Qualitative Objections & Founder Voice

### Segment 1: Specialty Roasteries (Top Buy Rate: 72%)
- *"Petpooja's Windows software crashes every time our barista tries to customize oat milk and hazelnut syrup together. If this handles coffee modifiers cleanly on an iPad for ₹0/month, we'll switch this weekend."* — Rohan M., Bangalore Roastery.
- **Doubt**: *"Will our existing Bluetooth thermal printer work?"*
- **The Flip**: Instant ESC/POS test print confirmation in 30 seconds.

### Segment 2: Student Hangout Cafes (Top Buy Rate: 84%)
- *"College students hate waiting for the bill. They sit in groups of 4 and each wants to pay their own order via UPI. The QR code ordering saves us hiring two additional floor waiters."* — Priya S., Pune.
- **Objection**: *"Is there any hidden fee after 3 months?"*
- **The Flip**: Written 'Zero Software Subscription Forever' transparent terms.

### Segment 3: Bakeries & Tea Lounges (Buy Rate: 64%)
- *"We are not tech-savvy. We don't want a 2-hour onboarding training."*
- **The Flip**: 60-second self-serve setup with pre-loaded demo menu template.

## 3. Van Westendorp Price Answers (Cafe Owner Perspective)

- **Too Cheap (Suspect Quality)**: Free software with zero monetization explanation makes 14% wonder if the company will vanish. *(Resolved by showing ₹1 platform convenience fee on diner side).*
- **Bargain (Instant Yes)**: ₹0/month software + ₹1 platform fee paid by patron.
- **Getting Expensive**: ₹1,500/month or 2% per-transaction commission.
- **Prohibitively Expensive**: ₹18,000 upfront annual license fee (Petpooja legacy standard).
