# BrewPluse - Modern Cafe Operating System & Intelligent Cloud POS

- **What it is**: An all-in-one cloud POS, contactless QR table ordering system, live Kitchen Display System (KDS), thermal billing, and customer dining mobile app with a disruptive ₹0/month software subscription model for cafes.
- **Who it is for**: Independent specialty coffee shops, artisan cafes, bakeries, tea rooms, quick-service restaurants (QSRs), and dining patrons across India and tier 1-2 emerging metro markets.
- **What it sells, at what price**:
  - Core POS & Operations Software: ₹0/month (Free forever for cafes).
  - Platform Convenience Fee: Flat ₹1 per order paid seamlessly on digital QR table orders.
  - Physical Hardware & Merchandising: Smart Acrylic NFC & QR Table Stands (₹199 - ₹499/unit), 2-inch & 3-inch Bluetooth/WiFi Thermal Receipt Printers (₹2,499 - ₹4,999/unit), Social Review Booster NFC Cards.
- **Where and how**: Delivered 100% online as an instant-setup cloud SaaS (Web on Next.js 15, responsive PWA, and native Android APK), self-service onboarding in under 60 seconds with QR generator and menu import tools.
- **Budget and constraints**: Bootstrapped and lean; low server overhead on edge infrastructure; offline-first local cache fallback for zero network downtime; payment routing via UPI and Razorpay.
