# BrewPluse Video Production & Editing Playbook
*Lead Video Producer & Content Editor*

## 1. Core Video Asset Categories

1. **Category A: The 30-Second B2B Problem/Solution Reel (9:16 Vertical)**
   - Target: Cafe owners scrolling Instagram/LinkedIn at night.
   - Format: Jump-cut, high-contrast talking head or over-the-shoulder POV.
2. **Category B: The 60-Second Full Product Walkthrough (16:9 Landscape)**
   - Target: YouTube, website demo page (`/demo`), and sales outreach.
   - Format: Direct screen-recording with kinetic highlight boxes and smooth pan-zooms.
3. **Category C: The 15-Second Dining Experience Hook (9:16 Vertical)**
   - Target: Patrons & foodies on Instagram Stories / TikTok.
   - Format: Phone tapping table acrylic card &rarr; smooth menu glide &rarr; iced coffee served.

---

## 2. Audio & Visual Editing Standards

- **Pacing & Dead Space Cutting**:
  - Remove all silences longer than 0.3 seconds.
  - Cut retakes and vocal filler ("um", "ah", "basically").
  - 1 visual punch-in (1.15x scale) every 3 to 4 seconds to maintain ocular engagement.
- **Word-Timed Captions**:
  - Font: Bold sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Montserrat ExtraBold`).
  - Style: Active word highlighted in Crema Gold (`#B99A5B`) on Deep Forest Green backdrop (`#002D23`).
  - Maximum 3 words on screen at a time to prevent cognitive overload.
- **Audio Mastering & Loudness**:
  - Voiceover Target: **-14 LUFS** integrated loudness (standard for Reels & Shorts).
  - Background Music (BGM): Lofi cafe jazz or subtle vinyl crackle dipped by **-16 dB** under voiceover (auto-ducking).
  - SFX: Crisp tactile click when buttons are tapped; gentle metallic bell chime when the order reaches the kitchen.
