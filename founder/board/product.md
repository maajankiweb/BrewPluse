# The Product Lens: BrewPluse Review
*Framework: Walter Isaacson, Steve Jobs & Apple Product Discipline*

## 1. Focus: The Power of Saying "No"
- **What BrewPluse Refuses to Build on Day 1**: Complex liquor excise tax tracking, multi-warehouse shipping logistics, hotel room billing, catering event ERP.
- **The Core Mastery**: The perfect beverage and cafe dining journey. Barista grind-and-pour queue, milk choice toggles (Oat/Almond/Whole), sugar level steppers, instant table order dispatch, and 1-tap UPI bills.

## 2. Start from the Experience and Work Back
- **The Dining Moment**: A guest sits at Table 4. They tap their phone or scan the acrylic card. The menu glides open with crisp photography and dish stories. They customize their flat white with oat milk, tap Pay via UPI, and the kitchen printer buzzes while the barista's KDS screen chimes with a clean two-tone alert.
- **The Cafe Owner Moment**: No ugly bulky touchscreen PC with tangled cables at the counter. Just an iPad or Android tablet on a sleek wooden stand.

## 3. End-to-End Craftsmanship
- Warm cream linen (`#F7F5EC`), rich forest green (`#002D23`), and warm gold typography replace sterile white/blue corporate tables.
- Audio alert: Self-synthesized harmonic Web Audio chime (C6 - 1046 Hz & G5 - 784 Hz) instead of harsh buzzing alarms.
- Dedicated customer dining app completely stripped of owner dashboard clutter for a focused, distraction-free ordering flow.

## Score & Vote
- **Score**: 9.2 / 10
- **Vote**: **FUND**
- **Notes**: Exceptional product taste and cohesive mobile/web design language.
