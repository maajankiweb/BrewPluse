### **Consensus Decision: FUND WITH CONDITIONS (2-0-1 Majority)**

#### The Fatal Risks Identified:
1. **Fee Avoidance**: Cafe owners taking cash counter orders instead of QR orders to avoid the ₹1 convenience fee.
2. **Support Overhead**: Unassisted self-onboarded cafe owners struggling with thermal printer Bluetooth pairing.

#### Actionable Board Directives:
1. Provide cafe owners with a toggle in Dashboard: *"Cafe Pays ₹1 per order in bulk wallet"* vs *"Patron Pays ₹1 on QR checkout"*.
2. Provide a 1-click thermal printer test and instant WhatsApp setup concierge for hardware customers.
