# BrewPluse Board Briefing Memo

## Executive Summary
BrewPluse is a cloud-based cafe operating system, intelligent POS, and patron digital dining platform. It disrupts the traditional restaurant software market (which charges ₹15,000–₹50,000/year upfront plus commission) by offering a **₹0/month software subscription** to cafe owners, monetized via a **flat ₹1 per order convenience fee** on digital table orders and optional hardware sales.

## The Core Product
1. **Intelligent Cloud POS**: Touch billing, split checks, cash/UPI counter orders, thermal receipt printing, offline cache recovery.
2. **Contactless QR Table Ordering**: Digital menu with real-time dish stories, modifiers, allergy filters, customer ordering, and direct UPI payment.
3. **Live Kitchen Display System (KDS)**: Station-routed ticket line, countdown timers, prep status updates, and audio bell chime.
4. **Dedicated Customer Dining Android App**: Seamless mobile app for patrons with instant ordering, live kitchen tracking, and zero cafe owner dashboard clutter.
5. **Business Automation**: Recipe inventory deduction, waiter calling, Google review NFC boosters, digital stamp cards, and multi-location management.

## Business Model & Economics
- **Software Subscription**: ₹0/month (zero software licensing fee, zero monthly retainers).
- **Transaction Revenue**: ₹1 flat platform convenience fee charged on customer QR orders.
- **Hardware Margins**: Smart acrylic QR/NFC table cards (65% margin), Thermal printers (30% margin).
- **Target Market**: Over 250,000 independent cafes, coffee shops, bakeries, and QSRs across India and South Asia.
