## 1. Executive Summary
**BrewPluse** is a modern, cloud-native Cafe Operating System and Intelligent POS that eliminates the predatory ₹15,000–₹45,000 annual upfront subscription fees charged by legacy restaurant software companies (Petpooja, Posist, Toast). 

By offering a **₹0/month software subscription** to cafe owners and monetizing via a micro-scale **₹1 platform convenience fee per digital QR table order** alongside high-margin physical hardware (acrylic NFC stands and thermal printers), BrewPluse creates an irresistible acquisition flywheel.

---

## 2. Market Problem & Strategic Gap
- **Acute Industry Pain**: Over 250,000 independent cafes and QSRs in India operate on thin 12–18% net margins. Traditional POS vendors lock them into expensive annual software licenses and charge per terminal.
- **The BrewPluse Monopoly Gap**:
  - Zero upfront software cost & zero monthly fees.
  - 60-second self-serve onboarding from any phone or browser.
  - Specialized beverage logic (milk varieties, roast styles, sugar levels, ice temperatures).
  - Dedicated patron dining mobile app with real-time KDS tracking, completely separated from cashier dashboard screens.

---
