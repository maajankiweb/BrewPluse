## 2. Fixed Overhead & Break-Even Analysis

- **Total Fixed Monthly Overhead**: **₹15,000 / month**
  - Cloud infrastructure & domain: ₹3,000
  - Support & ticketing tools: ₹2,000
  - Operations & community maintenance: ₹10,000
- **Daily Break-Even Volume**:
  $$\text{Break-Even Orders/Day} = \frac{₹15,000}{₹0.82 \times 30} \approx \mathbf{610\text{ orders/day}}$$
- **Active Cafes Needed to Break Even**:
  At an average of 75 QR orders per cafe/day, BrewPluse achieves operational break-even with just **8 to 9 active cafes**!
