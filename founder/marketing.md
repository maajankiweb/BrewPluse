# BrewPluse Go-To-Market & 30-Day Launch Campaign
*Director of Marketing Strategy*

## 1. Positioning Statement
*"For independent cafe and coffee shop owners who are tired of paying ₹25,000/year to clunky legacy POS companies, BrewPluse is a modern cloud operating system that provides contactless QR ordering, live KDS, and instant billing for ₹0/month."*

---

## 2. Acquisition Channels That Actually Work

1. **Direct Walk-In Cafe Visits (High Conversion)**: Visiting 10 cafes daily in dense cafe hubs (Koramangala, Indiranagar, Bandra, Cyber Hub). Showing the live demo on an iPad in 45 seconds.
2. **Viral "Powered by BrewPluse" Table Loops**: Every guest sitting at a table scans the QR code. Over 100+ diners per day per cafe interact with the brand.
3. **Instagram & Reels for Cafe Founders**: Short-form clips showing the pain of manual paper KOT orders vs sleek iPad KDS.
4. **WhatsApp Cafe Groups & Communities**: Specialty coffee roaster associations, home-brewer forums, and local F&B entrepreneur groups.

---

## 3. Top 10 High-Converting Hooks

1. *"Why are you still paying ₹25,000 a year for POS software when your coffee beans already cost enough?"*
2. *"We built an entire cafe POS system that costs ₹0/month. Here’s why we did it."*
3. *"The exact reason 4 out of 5 cafes lose ₹40,000 every month on slow table turnover."*
4. *"Watch our barista take, customize, and route an oat-milk cortado to the kitchen in 4 seconds flat."*
5. *"Stop making your customers wave their hands for 10 minutes just to get the bill."*
6. *"How to get 50+ 5-star Google reviews every week without begging your customers."*
7. *"Paper KOT slips vs iPad Kitchen Display: A side-by-side kitchen rush test."*
8. *"No Windows desktop, no tangled printer cables. Run your entire cafe from an iPad."*
9. *"What happens when your cafe internet drops in the middle of Sunday brunch rush? (Offline mode demo)."*
10. *"How one Indiranagar cafe saved 1.5 waiter salaries by switching to QR table ordering."*

---

## 4. 30-Day Launch Campaign Schedule

- **Week 1 (Days 1–7): The Seed 10 Cafes**
  - Personal outreach and walk-ins to 10 friendly local cafes in the founder's immediate city.
  - Setup their menus and install complimentary acrylic QR table stands.
  - Goal: 10 live cafes processing at least 300 orders/day.
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14): Proof & Case Studies**
  - Record short 30-second customer testimonial reels from the first 5 cafe owners.
  - Publish on Instagram and LinkedIn with the *"Before BrewPluse vs After BrewPluse"* format.
  - Goal: 25 organic inbound signups.
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21): Hardware Drop & Community Launch**
  - Launch the Smart NFC & QR Table Stand bundles on the website (`/download` & hardware shop).
  - Launch founder-led Twitter/X thread and Product Hunt announcement.
  - Goal: 50 active cafes.
- **Week 4 (Days 22–30): Referral Engine & Expansion**
  - Launch the "Refer a Fellow Cafe" incentive: Give both cafes ₹500 in free hardware credits.
  - Reach first 100 onboarded cafes.
